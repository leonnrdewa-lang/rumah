### What "public domain (as tagged)" means, and what you should still check
* Commons tags these files as public domain because they are machine-generated output without a human author (the "algorithmic" tag). That is a
  statement about copyright law in the United States; other countries may treat AI output differently, and the model provider's own terms of service
  are a separate layer. The reel uses them as short, attributed, editorial examples in a piece *about* those models. If you monetise the video
  or work with a brand, have someone confirm this for your situation.
* **Five clips are CC BY-SA 4.0** (Lwneal, Benlisquare, Oronbb x3). Attribution is required, and ShareAlike may apply to the adapted footage (they are cropped,
  graded and cut). Credit them in the post caption (text below). If you would rather not carry ShareAlike, replace them: edit
  `src/footage_sources.json` (ids `e_gen2_dog`, `e_sd_park`, `e_2022_a`, `e_2022_b`, `e_gen1`) and re-run the pipeline.
* No audio from any source clip is used; the sound is the narration plus the original score below.

## 2. Sound
* **Score and every sound effect**: synthesised from scratch by `src/synth_audio.py` (numpy, no samples, no libraries). Nothing to license. It is a deliberate device: the
  arpeggio starts bit-crushed and low-rate and resolves to full fidelity as the story reaches the present.
* **Narration**: 11 takes generated with **ByteDance Seed Audio 1.0** through the Higgsfield `generate_audio` tool (voice preset "Arthur"), cost about 0.4 credit per take.
  Check that your Higgsfield plan permits commercial use of generated speech.
* **ElevenLabs v4 was NOT used** (see README). `src/tts_elevenlabs.py` regenerates the takes with your own ElevenLabs key.
