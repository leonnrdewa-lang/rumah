# DIRECTION — "FROM MELTING PIXELS TO MOVING WORLDS."

Creative bible for the 120-second vertical film. Every sequence author follows it. Nothing here overrides the licensing and honesty rules in BRIEF.md.

## 1. Idea
**The frame is a material.** At first the frame is unstable matter: pixels melt, identities smear, frames crumble into fragments. Over the film the frame *locks*: the same subject survives from frame to frame, light behaves, cameras move on purpose, and finally the frame becomes a world you can move through. The whole film is one continuous act of the frame solidifying.

Recurring device — **the Spine**: one hairline timeline (a horizontal line with year ticks) that appears in every chapter and *transforms* with the story:
| Chapter | Spine state |
|---|---|
| S1 hook 0-8 | cracked: segments displaced and jittering; snaps straight (locks) on the smash cut |
| S2 early experiments 8-25 | grows from scattered fragmentary frame-icons into a continuous line; ticks appear 2016..2022 |
| S3 first leap 25-43 | becomes a spatial corridor - the camera dives along the time axis, milestone panels stand in depth (parallax) |
| S4 race 43-63 | unfolds into a moving gallery / conveyor of panels, one lane per developer family |
| S5 control 63-83 | bends into camera-path curves (dolly, orbit, crane) and reference/keyframe connectors - diagram mode |
| S6 best examples 83-104 | recedes to a thin bar with a playhead; minimal overlay on hero shots |
| S7 payoff 104-115 | collapses to a single point that detonates into the multi-panel montage, which collapses into one full-screen shot |
| S8 final 115-120 | closes into a loop and lands on a still that matches frame 0 of the film (replay seam) |

## 2. Identity
* Colour: **charcoal** #0F1012 (background), **warm white** #F4EFE4 (type, hairlines), **volt** #D2FF3C (the single vivid accent: "locked / now / verified"), grey #8A8780 (secondary). No other saturated colours in the graphics. Early footage is graded slightly desaturated and cool-grey; recent footage neutral and clean; volt marks moments of stability.
* Type: **Anton** (display, all caps, tight), **Instrument Serif Italic** (editorial emphasis words), **JetBrains Mono** (labels, years, credits), **Inter 800** (subtitles). Large hierarchy: one hero word or phrase per beat (>= 170 px display), labels 22-28 px mono.
* Safe zones (1080x1920): side margins 64 px; nothing essential above y = 210 or below y = 1500; subtitles occupy y 1250-1420. The spine sits at y ~ 176-236 (decorative; years below it at y >= 245).
* **Source label** (every excerpt, always): line 1 `MODEL · YEAR` (mono bold 24), line 2 `creator · licence` (mono 18, grey). Placed bottom-left of the panel or under it, never covering a watermark. For CC BY / CC BY-SA the creator name is part of the label. Comparisons across different prompts/conditions carry a small `ILLUSTRATIVE - DIFFERENT PROMPTS` tag.
* **Panels**: uncropped source frame (fit, never stretched), 4 px charcoal inset, hairline outline, four registration ticks; drop shadow on charcoal; rounded 6 px corners. Full-bleed cover crops are allowed only for clips profiled as overlay-free in the cropped region (see footage profile `overlays`).
* **Comparison frames**: same geometry for both sides, year plates `2023` / `2026` in Anton, a volt hairline divider that can slide.
* Grain: 6-9 (early), 2-3 (recent). Vignette 0.3. No lens flares, no cheap glitch packs, no random zooms.

## 3. Motion language
* Every element: **anticipation -> movement -> controlled settle** (`gfx.kinetic`). Typical timing 0.45 s; hero type 0.6 s; cuts on beats.
* Depth: labels move at 1.0x, panels at ~0.6x, background lines at ~0.2x (parallax). Purposeful motion blur (sub-frame accumulation) on fast translations only.
* Rhythm: fast burst (0.3-0.8 s) -> readable hold (1.5-3 s) -> long hero (3-5 s). Never stack effects to fill a beat. Overlays vanish during hero shots.
* Transitions are content-linked: a panel becomes the next panel (match geometry), letterforms of the year numeral mask the next footage, slat masks follow the spine direction, shards for "melting" -> "locking".

## 5. Sound language
Music arc: strange sparse opening (drone + detuned intervals) -> rhythmic momentum through the history -> strong energy in the gallery -> cinematic lift for hero shots -> controlled resolution. Sound design is event-driven from the same cue sheet as the picture: frame **locks** (short tonal click + sub), panel moves (soft whoosh scaled to distance), timeline **jumps** (pitched sweep), typographic impacts (low hit + air), year changes (tick), transitions (texture matched to the transition), freeze frame (everything drops out, 0.3-0.4 s of near silence). Music ducks under narration (~ -12 dB); the final mix is -14 LUFS, true peak <= -1 dBTP.

## 6. Honesty rules for graphics
No fabricated metrics, benchmarks, percentages or "quality scores". Any comparison is labelled ILLUSTRATIVE and, if prompts/conditions differ, says so. Annotations describe only what is measurable from the clip itself (resolution, fps, duration, measured flicker/ghosting) or stated on the source page. Reconstructions (if any) are labelled `RECONSTRUCTION` and never presented as historical footage.
